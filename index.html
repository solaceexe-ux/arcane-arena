HOW TO PLAY
🎮 THE 3 BASICS
1 · MOVE — press WASD (or the Arrow keys) to walk around the arena.
2 · AIM & SHOOT — move the mouse to aim at things, then hold the LEFT mouse button. Your hero blasts spells automatically while held.
3 · SUPERPOWER — press SPACE. Every hero has their own huge magic power, and it fires exactly where your mouse points.
4 · ★ SPECIAL SKILL — press E any time (recharges every 4 seconds): your hero flashes their unique emote + dialogue, hits nearby enemies for 10% of their max HP plus their own skill effect — 🔥Blaze burns · ⚡Volt & 🌪Gale surge damage · ❄Frost ice-armors · 🌑Shadow blinks untouchable · 🪨Terra shockwaves enemies away · ☀Sol heals 25% · ☠Venom drops a toxic cloud — and a 🛡 shield protects you during the flash!
⚡ THE POWER BUTTON
Look at the ⚡ circle in the top-left HUD (next to your health bar) — that is your power button.
• Glowing & bright = power is READY → smash SPACE!
• Dark = cooling down — a shadow sweeps around the circle as the countdown ticks.
Powers are the strongest thing you have — don't save them, USE THEM! 💥

💡 SURVIVAL TIPS
❤️ Hearts heal +30  ·  ⭐ Stars double your damage for 7s. Grab them before they vanish!
👹 Every 5th wave, the ARENA TITAN boss shows up. Keep moving!
🏠 Cover matters: nets, houses, poles and crystals block bullets (yours AND the enemies') — hide behind them and peek out. Grass slows walkers but bullets fly over it.
⌨️ Quick keys: ESC or the red ✕ = exit to menu  ·  P = pause  ·  M = music on/off.
🌐 PLAYING WITH A FRIEND
Same WiFi: host picks PLAY WITH FRIEND → HOST → SAME WiFi, friend opens http://host-ip:8642 → JOIN with the code.
★ Different WiFi (any distance!): host picks HOST → 🌍 DIFFERENT WiFi, then sends the friend the game file once (WhatsApp / Drive). The friend opens that file in a browser, picks JOIN and types the code — the game world instantly livestreams through it!
You both fight on the same map as heroes — both can press SPACE for powers, share hearts, and a fallen player revives between waves!
